Return & Refund Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

We want you to absolutely love your Mega Store purchase. If something isn't right — a size that doesn't fit, a different colour than expected, or in the rare case a defective item — this detailed policy explains exactly what you can return or exchange, within how many days, and precisely how the process works from your first message to the money back in your account. Please read it once; it will make every future return quick and painless.

1. Return Eligibility — The 4 Conditions

A return request is accepted when all four of the following conditions are met:

  1. Time limit: The request is raised within 7 days of delivery. The day the parcel is delivered counts as Day 0. For example, if your parcel arrives on Monday, you have until the following Monday to raise the request.
  2. Unused condition: The item is completely unused, unwashed, unsoiled and has not been worn outside.
  3. Tags & packaging intact: All original tags, labels, buttons, spare buttons and the original packaging/polybag are still attached and undamaged.
  4. No alteration or damage: The item shows no signs of alteration, perfume/deodorant marks, stains, or damage caused after delivery.
Quick quality check before requesting: Please inspect your parcel as soon as it arrives. Try the item on over your own clothing (to avoid makeup/perfume transfer), keep the tags on, and keep the packaging until you are sure you want to keep it.

2. Non-Returnable Items — Please Note Carefully

  • Innerwear and intimate apparel — for hygiene reasons, these cannot be returned or exchanged under any circumstances, unless the item itself is defective.
  • Products explicitly marked as "Final Sale" or "Non-Returnable" on their product page.
  • Items damaged due to misuse, wrong washing (not following the care label), ironing at excessive heat, or normal wear and tear.
  • Free gifts, samples or accessories included in a bundle.

3. Size Issues & Exchanges — Step by Step

Wrong size happens to everyone. Here is exactly how an exchange works:

  1. Check the size chart first: Before ordering, always compare your body measurements with the size chart on the product page. This single habit prevents most size issues.
  2. Message us within 7 days of delivery on WhatsApp at 8134958144 with your Order ID and tell us the size you need (for example: "Order MS-123456, need M instead of L").
  3. We check stock of the requested size in the same colour. Exchanges are subject to availability.
  4. Dispatch of the new size: Once confirmed, we ship the replacement to the same address. In most cases we ask you to return the old size first; our team will share the simple return instructions.
  5. Receive your new size — typically within 7–10 business days, the same as a normal order.

Important: If you want a different product (not just a different size), that is treated as a return + fresh order — the returned item goes through the refund process in Section 6, and you can place a new order anytime.

4. Wrong, Defective or Damaged Items — 48-Hour Window

If you receive an item that is wrong (different product/colour/size than ordered), defective (tear, broken zip, misprint, stitching defect) or damaged in transit, we take full responsibility. Follow these steps:

  1. Contact us within 48 hours of delivery — this window helps us raise the courier claim in time, so please don't delay.
  2. Send clear photos or a short unboxing-style video showing the issue, the product label/tag, and the packaging. Visual proof makes approval almost instant.
  3. Keep the original packaging until your claim is fully resolved — the courier may need to inspect it.
  4. Choose your resolution: a free replacement of the same item, or a full refund — your choice, no questions asked.

For approved wrong/defective/damaged claims, we also refund any shipping charges you paid, because the fault was ours.

5. How to Request a Return — The Complete Process Step by Step

  1. Open WhatsApp and message us at 8134958144 (or call if you prefer speaking).
  2. Share your Order ID — found in "My Orders" or your confirmation message — and state clearly that you want to return the item.
  3. Tell us the reason: size issue, changed mind, colour difference, or defect. For defects, attach photos/video at this stage.
  4. Wait for eligibility confirmation: Our team checks the 7-day window and item condition criteria, usually within minutes during business hours.
  5. Receive return instructions: We share the return address and packing steps. Depending on your PIN code, we arrange a doorstep pickup or ask you to self-ship the item to us.
  6. Pack the item correctly: Fold the item neatly, keep all tags attached, place it in its original packaging or a secure polybag, and include a slip of paper with your Order ID and registered phone number inside the parcel.
  7. Hand over the parcel to the pickup agent or courier, and share the tracking/AWB number with us if you self-shipped.
  8. Inspection at our warehouse (1–2 business days): Once the parcel arrives, our quality team verifies that it meets the return conditions of Section 1.
  9. Refund or exchange is processed immediately after approval — you receive a confirmation message on WhatsApp.

7. Return Rejection — When and Why

A returned item may be sent back to you (at your cost) or the refund declined if, upon inspection, it is found to be:

  • Used, washed, soiled, or smelling of perfume/deodorant;
  • Missing tags, packaging, or accessories included with the product;
  • Damaged after delivery due to misuse or incorrect washing;
  • A non-returnable category item (Section 2).

We always share photographic evidence from our inspection team so the decision is transparent and fair.

8. Cancellation Before Delivery

Remember — if the order hasn't shipped yet, cancelling it is faster and simpler than returning it. Full instructions are on our Cancellation Policy page.

9. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Who pays the return shipping cost?

For wrong/defective/damaged items — we do (free replacement or refund, shipping included). For change-of-mind or size exchanges, the return shipping may be borne by the customer; our team will tell you the exact arrangement for your PIN code before you confirm.

Can I exchange for a different colour or product?

That counts as a return + new order. The original item is refunded per Section 6, and you place a fresh order for the product you want.

I missed the 7-day window by one day. Can anything be done?

Message us anyway. While the policy limit is 7 days, we review genuine cases (for example a delivery you only received late) on a case-by-case basis.

How will I know my refund is done?

We send a confirmation on WhatsApp, and the amount appears in your account/UPI within the stated timeline. COD refunds show as a direct transfer to the UPI ID you shared with us.

Do I need the original box/polybag?

The original polybag/packaging with tags is strongly recommended — items returned without original tags or packaging may fail the quality check.
