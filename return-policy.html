Return & Refund Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

We want you to love your purchase. If something isn't right, our return policy below explains how we can help.

1. Return Eligibility

  • Return requests must be raised within 7 days of delivery.
  • Items must be unused, unwashed, with original tags and packaging intact.
  • Items must not be damaged, soiled or altered in any way.

2. Non-Returnable Items

  • Innerwear and intimate apparel (for hygiene reasons).
  • Items marked as "Final Sale" or "Non-Returnable" on the product page.
  • Products damaged due to misuse, washing contrary to care instructions, or normal wear and tear.

3. Size Issues & Exchanges

  • If the size doesn't fit, you may request an exchange for a different size, subject to stock availability.
  • Please check the Size Chart on the product page before ordering to reduce the chance of size mismatch.
  • Exchanges for a different product (not just size) are treated as a return + new order.

4. Wrong, Defective or Damaged Items

  • If you receive a wrong, defective or damaged item, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos/videos.
  • We will arrange a free replacement or full refund after verification.
  • Please keep the original packaging until your claim is resolved.

5. How to Request a Return

  1. Message us on WhatsApp at 8134958144 with your Order ID, reason and photos (if applicable).
  2. Our support team will confirm eligibility and share pickup/return instructions.
  3. Once we receive and inspect the returned item, your refund or exchange will be processed.

6. Refunds

  • Online payments: Refund is issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after inspection approval.
  • Cash on Delivery orders: Refund is issued via UPI or bank transfer within 5–7 business days after approval.
  • Shipping charges (if any) are refundable only for wrong/defective item claims.

7. Cancellation Before Delivery

You may cancel an order before it is shipped. Please see our Cancellation Policy for details.
