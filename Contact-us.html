Business Hours & Response Times — Step by Step

  1. WhatsApp and Call support is available 7 days a week. We keep the lines open every day because we know orders and deliveries do not stop on weekends.
  2. Messages received outside active hours (late night or very early morning) are queued and answered first thing the next morning, usually within the first hour of the day.
  3. During big sale events (festival sales, clearance events) response times can stretch to a few hours due to high volume. We sincerely appreciate your patience — every single message is read and answered.
  4. If you don't get a reply within a few hours during business hours, please re-send your message. Occasionally a message gets buried — a gentle follow-up always reaches us.

Before You Contact Us — Preparation Checklist

Following these simple steps helps us resolve your query in a single conversation instead of several back-and-forth messages:

  1. Keep your Order ID ready. You can find it in "My Orders" on the website, or in the confirmation message you received after checkout. It looks like MS-123456.
  2. For return or defect claims, attach a clear photo or a short video of the item — showing the issue clearly (for example a tear, stain, wrong colour, or missing part). Claims with visual proof are approved much faster.
  3. Mention the product name and size if your question is about a specific product you haven't ordered yet.
  4. State your question in one clear sentence at the start of the message — for example: "I want to exchange size M for size L in order MS-123456."
  5. Stay on the same chat thread. This preserves your conversation history and helps any of our team members pick up right where the last one left off.

Shipping From & Delivery Information

  • All orders are packed and shipped from our warehouse in Assam, India. Every parcel is quality-checked and sealed before dispatch.
  • Standard delivery takes 7–10 business days across India, depending on your destination PIN code. Metro cities are usually on the faster end; remote pin codes may take the full duration.
  • Tracking details are updated inside "My Orders" as soon as the courier scans the parcel, and we also share dispatch updates on WhatsApp where possible.
  • Please keep your phone reachable on the delivery day — the courier may call to confirm your address or directions.

Common Questions — Quick Answers

Where is my order?

Open "My Orders" and tap your order to see the live status and tracking link. If the tracking hasn't moved for 4+ days, message us with your Order ID and we will raise a trace with the courier.

How do I cancel my order?

Go to "My Orders" and tap "Cancel Order" while the status is Pending/Processing. Full step-by-step instructions are on our Cancellation Policy page.

How do I return or exchange an item?

Message us on WhatsApp at 8134958144 with your Order ID and photos (if defective). The complete process is explained in our Return Policy.

My payment failed but money was deducted. What now?

Don't worry — failed-payment amounts are auto-reversed by the bank/payment gateway within 5–7 business days. If it takes longer, share the transaction screenshot with us and we will escalate it.

Do you deliver to my PIN code?

We deliver across India. If your PIN code is serviceable by our courier partners, checkout will proceed normally. For any doubt, just ask us on WhatsApp before ordering.

How can I give feedback or a suggestion?

We genuinely love hearing from customers. Message us on WhatsApp or Instagram — good or bad, your feedback shapes what we do next.
