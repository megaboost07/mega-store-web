2. How We Use Your Information — Step by Step

  1. Order processing: Your name, address and phone number are used to generate shipping labels and courier manifests so your parcel reaches the right doorstep.
  2. Communication: We use your phone number to send order confirmations, shipping updates and delivery coordination messages via SMS or WhatsApp.
  3. Customer support: Our team accesses your order history when you contact us, so you never have to repeat your story to every agent.
  4. Refunds: For return/refund processing, we use your payment details (as provided by the gateway) or your UPI/bank details (for COD refunds) to send the money back.
  5. Improvement: Aggregated, non-identifiable browsing patterns help us understand which products and pages customers love, so we can stock better and design a smoother website.
  6. Marketing (optional): With your consent, we may send new arrivals, restock alerts and exclusive offers. You can opt out at any time, free of charge.

3. How We Store & Protect Your Data

  • Your order and account data is stored on Firebase (Google Cloud infrastructure), which provides enterprise-grade security with encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest.
  • Cart items, wishlist, theme and language preferences are stored in your browser's local storage — they stay on your device and are never transmitted to our servers.
  • Access to customer data inside our company is restricted to team members who need it for order processing or support, under confidentiality obligations.
  • We never store card numbers, CVVs or UPI PINs — payment data is handled entirely by PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways.
  • While we follow industry-standard safeguards, no method of internet transmission is 100% secure. We encourage you to keep your phone and accounts protected and to never share OTPs with anyone.

4. How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Order records: Kept for a minimum of the period required under Indian tax and commercial law (typically several years), for accounting and legal compliance.
  • Support chats: Retained for up to 12 months to help with follow-ups and dispute resolution, after which they may be archived or deleted.
  • Local browser data: Retained on your device until you clear your browser cache/site data — you control this fully.
  • When data is no longer needed, it is securely deleted or irreversibly anonymised.

5. Sharing of Information — Who Sees Your Data and Why

We want to be crystal clear: we never sell, trade or rent your personal information to anyone for marketing purposes. Your data is shared only in these limited situations:

  • Courier & logistics partners: Your name, delivery address and phone number are shared solely to physically deliver your parcel.
  • Payment gateways: When you pay online, your payment details are processed directly by the gateway (e.g., Razorpay/PayU/etc.). They receive only what is necessary to complete the transaction, under their own strict privacy and PCI-DSS compliance.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect our rights, property or safety, or that of our customers.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website uses browser local storage (a technology similar to cookies) to remember the following on your device:

  • Your shopping cart contents — so items stay in your cart even if you close the browser.
  • Your wishlist items — so your saved favourites are there when you return.
  • Your theme preference (light or dark mode) — so the site always looks the way you like it.
  • Your language selection — so you don't have to re-select it every visit.

You can clear all of this data at any time through your browser settings (usually under "Privacy" → "Clear browsing data" → "Cookies and site data"). Clearing it will reset your cart, wishlist and theme.

7. Your Rights Over Your Data

You are always in control of your personal information. You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Ask us to fix any inaccurate or incomplete details (like a misspelt name or outdated address).
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements (e.g., completed orders must be kept for accounting purposes).
  • Opt-out: Stop receiving promotional messages at any time by replying "STOP" or asking us on WhatsApp.

To exercise any of these rights, follow these steps:

  1. Message us on WhatsApp at 8134958144 from the same number used on your order.
  2. Clearly state your request — for example: "Please send me a copy of my data" or "Please correct my delivery address".
  3. We verify your identity using your Order ID / registered number, then process most requests within 7–10 business days.

8. Children's Privacy

If you believe a child has provided us data without parental consent, contact us and we will delete it promptly.
